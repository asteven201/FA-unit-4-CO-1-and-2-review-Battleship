How to play

Two players take turns on the same device. Each player has a hidden fleet of 5 ships. On your turn, solve a new math question to earn one shot, then choose a square on your opponent's ocean. A correct answer earns a shot; an incorrect answer ends your turn. Sink all 5 ships to win!

For graphing, click two different grid points on the line. For writing equations, type an answer such as y=2x+3 or y=-1/2x+4.
